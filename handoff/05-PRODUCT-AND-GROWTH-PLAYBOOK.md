# 05 — Indore Launch & Growth Playbook

## 1. Pre-Launch Seeding Checklist (Done ✅)
Never share an empty discovery app. Nukti Poodi launches with **16 pre-seeded Indore Bhandara hotspots**:
- Khajrana Ganesh Mandir, Ranjeet Hanuman, Bada Ganpati, Annapurna Mata Annakshetra, Sai Mandir Scheme 54, Rajwada, Bhawarkua DAVV Gate, LIG Gurudwara, Pitreshwar Hanuman Dham, Sarafa Kanch Mandir, Palasia, Geeta Bhawan, Sudama Nagar, Nipania ISKCON, MR-10, and Bengali Square.

---

## 2. Week 1 Hyperlocal Growth Loops (Indore)
1. **WhatsApp Group Distribution:**
   - Every Bhandara card & detail page has a 1-tap `WhatsApp Share` button generating a crisp Hinglish message with Prasad Menu + Timing + Google Maps Pin + Nukti Poodi link.
   - Target groups: Bhawarkua hostel/coaching groups (huge student demand), Vijay Nagar IT/colony groups, Rajwada/Sarafa vyapari groups.
2. **Verified Mandir Program:**
   - Approach local temple committees (Khajrana, Ranjeet Hanuman, Annapurna, Geeta Bhawan) and offer them a free `✓ Verified Mandir` badge so devotees trust their timings.
3. **Festival Calendar Spikes:**
   - Run special campaigns on **Mangalwar/Shanivar** (Hanuman bhandaras), **Ekadashi**, **Navratri**, **Ram Navami**, **Hanuman Jayanti**, **Mahashivratri**, and **Ahilya Utsav**.
4. **Instagram Reels Hook:**
   - *"Indore me aaj kahan-kahan Nukti-Poodi aur Dal-Bafla ka bhandara chal raha hai? Google pe search karo ya link bio me hai!"*
